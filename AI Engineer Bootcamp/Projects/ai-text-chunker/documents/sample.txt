Artificial intelligence is changing the way modern
organizations build software and analyze information.
Machine learning allows systems to learn patterns from
data and make predictions without being explicitly
programmed for every possible situation.
Large language models are a type of AI system that can
process and generate natural language. They are used
for chatbots, summarization, document analysis, coding
assistants, and information retrieval.
Retrieval augmented generation combines language models
with external knowledge sources. Instead of relying only
on information learned during model training, a RAG
system can retrieve relevant information from documents
and provide that information to the language model.
Vector databases are commonly used to store embeddings.
Embeddings represent the semantic meaning of text as
numerical vectors. Similar pieces of information can
then be retrieved based on semantic similarity.
