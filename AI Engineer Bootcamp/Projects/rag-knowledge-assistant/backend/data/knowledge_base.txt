Work From Home Policy
Employees can work from home up to two days per week with prior approval from their manager. Employees working remotely must remain available during standard working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

Leave Policy
Employees should submit planned leave at least three working days in advance. Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

IT Support
Employees should provide screenshots, error messages, and relevant system information when reporting an IT issue.

Security Policy
Employees should report suspicious emails and potential security incidents to the security team immediately.
