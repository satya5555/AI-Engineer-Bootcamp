Company Working Hours
The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to complete their assigned work during these hours. Flexible working arrangements may be available depending on the team and manager.


Work From Home Policy
Employees can work from home up to two days per week with prior approval from their manager. Employees working remotely must remain available during standard working hours and attend scheduled meetings.


Leave Policy
Employees should submit planned leave requests at least three working days in advance. Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as possible. Leave approval depends on team requirements and available leave balance.


IT Support
Employees experiencing technical issues should contact the IT support team through the internal support portal. Critical system issues should be reported immediately. Employees should provide screenshots, error messages, and relevant system information when raising a technical issue.


Security Policy
Employees must never share their company passwords or authentication credentials. Sensitive company information should only be accessed through authorized systems. Suspicious emails or security incidents must be reported to the security team immediately.


Employee Benefits
Employees may be eligible for health insurance, retirement benefits, and other company-provided benefits depending on their employment category and company policy. Employees should contact HR for detailed information about their individual benefits.


Performance Reviews
Employee performance reviews are conducted periodically by managers. Reviews consider work quality, achievement of goals, collaboration, and professional development. Employees should discuss performance expectations with their managers.
