Company Working Hours

The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Work From Home Policy

Employees can work from home up to two days per week with prior approval from their manager. Employees working remotely must remain available during standard working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

Leave Policy

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least five working days in advance. Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

IT Support Policy

Employees experiencing technical issues should contact the IT support team through the internal support portal. Critical issues should be marked as high priority.

Security Policy

Employees must not share company credentials, passwords, or confidential information with unauthorized individuals.

Employee Benefits

Employees are eligible for health insurance and other benefits according to their employment category and company policies.

Performance Reviews

Employee performance reviews are conducted periodically. Managers provide feedback and discuss goals and development opportunities with employees.
